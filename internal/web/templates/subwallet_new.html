Add a sub-wallet

Sub-wallets are custodial. Their money stays on this node: you hold it, and you could take it. Tell each holder so. Someone who dislikes that can run their own node.

It starts with a balance of 0. Fund it from its page. It can pay, make invoices and read its own balance and history: a sub-wallet's permissions cannot be changed.
